Interior–exterior exposure and navigation

A viewer moves from a shaded interior toward a bright exterior and returns without losing the room’s focal direction or the sense of changing brightness.

What it will demonstrateA fixed exposure may crush the interior or clip the exit, while poorly controlled adaptation can pump, flatten the contrast, or make the transition distracting.

Presentation

Show the image, then show the reasoning.

A finished case should be understandable in a quick pass and still support a detailed technical conversation.

First view

Lead with the best relevant final image or short sequence, a clear role, and the project’s viewing context.

Decision view

Use saved cameras, stage isolations, and a concise explanation of why each source or change was needed.

Delivery view

Show one revision, identify actual assets and collaborators, and record the configuration behind the delivered image.
