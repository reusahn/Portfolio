02 / Unreal Engine · 2025–2026

Shininho / Jooan

Dusk lighting, character readability, and visual attention inside a 360° digital-human performance.

RoleTechnical Director
EngineUnreal Engine
LightingSkylight + HDRI dusk
ContextDigital human / VR performance
01 / Intent

Give the viewer a place to look.

In VR, the viewer can turn away from the performance. I used darkness, contrast, and a dusk environment to make the character easier to locate while keeping the viewer relatively stable.

Environment

A Skylight and HDRI-based dusk/sunset environment establish the scene.

Readability

Brightness and darkness direct attention toward the moving character.

Viewing

The staging allows the character to move through the environment without depending entirely on a fixed cinematic camera frame.

02 / Character & effects

Balance the performer and the hand-driven trails.

I built Niagara ribbon trails using separate emitters attached to both hands. Their visibility was timed to selected animation moments, and particle count was adjusted for the VR performance constraints.

Attachment

Separate emitters driven by the left and right hands.

Timing

Trail visibility changes at selected moments in the performance.

Constraint

Visual density and particle count balanced against the immersive runtime.

03 / Production scope

A lighting pass connected to the character’s identity.

In collaboration with artist Yaloo, the VR work extended the Shininho pipeline: scan cleanup, a Unity feasibility prototype, a production MetaHuman reconstruction, performance capture, and animation integration. My technical direction connected the character pipeline with the final real-time staging.

04 / Project footage

Review the immersive performance.

Original project footage provides the scene and motion context for the lighting, camera staging, and effects. This is full-project footage, not an isolated lighting-only reel.
