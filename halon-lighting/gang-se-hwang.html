03 / Unity HDRP · 2022

Gang Se-hwang

A reconstructed historical character, with skin, eyes, hair, and scene lighting developed as one real-time image.

RoleTechnical Director
EngineUnity HDRP
FocusCharacter lighting / lookdev
DeliveryPublic museum installation
01 / Rendering foundation

Adapt a proven human-rendering structure.

I used the structure of Unity’s contemporary digital-human rendering workflow, then adapted the character-specific geometry, maps, skin, eyes, hair, and final lighting around the reconstructed historical face.

Reused foundation

The existing Unity digital-human / Enemies-era material and rendering approach.

My contribution

Custom character reconstruction, character-specific material and surface tuning, lighting, animation integration, and the deployed Unity application.

Visual objective

Preserve the portrait-derived age and identity while making the face readable at the public viewing scale.

02 / Skin & light

Preserve detail inside the skin response.

Skin used HDRP subsurface scattering alongside roughness, specular, normal/detail-normal, and displacement information. I evaluated those maps under the final lighting so the scattering response did not wash out the reconstructed face’s age and surface character.

03 / Integration

Skin, eyes, and hair have to share the scene.

I tuned eye and corneal highlight response, hair-card shading, beard appearance, HDRI, scene lights, and final color response together. The aim was to make the separate rendering systems read as one person.

Eyes

Balance reflective and corneal highlights against the face lighting.

Hair & beard

Tune card-based shading and specular response within the same scene.

Final scene

Review the materials and lighting at the intended museum display scale.

04 / Delivery

A character that runs inside a public installation.

The final image was part of a live Unity application. I also implemented the camera composite, visitor interface, animation triggers, and interaction states that delivered the character to the public.

05 / Project footage

The finished character and installation.

Original project video shows the broader reconstruction, character performance, and installation context. Unity HDRP work is presented separately from the Unreal Engine cases.
