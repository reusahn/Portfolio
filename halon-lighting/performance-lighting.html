Room A + an existing character performance

01 / Situation

The lighting problem

A character turns, crosses a light boundary, and finishes a small performance beat while the lighting remains intentional.

Constraint

A strong still frame can conceal lost eye detail, unstable groom highlights, heavy shadow changes, and first-frame render artifacts.

Visual intention

Protect the emotional beat and face readability through the full movement rather than optimizing only the first or most flattering pose.

Scene setup

Use a short existing animation with a head turn and a small change of position. Lock one camera, save start / middle / end review frames, and use the same motion for all comparisons.

02 / Decisions

What will be changed, and why.

The work will be developed through controlled comparisons rather than a collection of unrelated looks.

Light the performance envelope

Review the character’s position and facial orientation over time before placing support sources. Identify the frames that genuinely need attention.

Preserve motivated transitions

Allow the character to move into a darker region when that fits the scene, while maintaining the intended expression and silhouette.

Separate viewport and delivery review

Inspect the real-time scene and the final image sequence independently. Document warm-up and render settings that matter to the actual scene rather than applying an unexplained preset.

05 / Revision exercise

Respond to a precise art-direction note.

This is a proposed revision exercise for the study, not feedback from an employer or a completed client engagement.

Proposed note
The starting frame works, but the eye sockets disappear halfway through the turn.

Planned response

Identify the affected range, compare a source-position change with targeted support, and re-check the whole motion so the correction does not damage the earlier frames.

Evidence to preserve

Keep the same camera or edited sequence, label the two versions, and write a short explanation of the visual change and its tradeoff.

06 / Technical review

Check the result under its intended viewing conditions.

Record the engine version and output mode used. Review real-time, headset, and offline-rendered material according to their actual delivery context.

  • Inspect the beginning of the rendered shot for unsettled effects or temporal history.
  • Watch for changing groom highlights, flickering shadows, and noisy indirect light.
  • Re-render the same camera after the fix and compare the exact frames.
