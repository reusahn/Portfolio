01 / Approach

Read the scene before placing the light.

Directing gives me a reason for the image: who holds our attention, how a room feels around them, and what changes when a person enters or leaves the frame. On-set lighting work gives those decisions a physical language of source direction, size, diffusion, color, and shadow.

Direction

Use framing, performance, and visual rhythm to communicate the emotional focus of a scene.

On-set experience

Music-video gaffer work adds practical familiarity with arranging and shaping physical light.

Real-time translation

Bring that visual reasoning into Unreal while accounting for character materials, shadow behavior, and the final camera or viewing context.

02 / Urban Diary

Domestic space, presence, and absence.

Urban Diary is an 18-minute live-action short co-directed by Jonghoon Ahn and Kosan. It follows a teenage girl’s changing experience of home after her father returns to work. The frames here show the film’s interior and exterior visual contexts.
