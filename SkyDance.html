Retarget drift.

Niagara · Blueprint · In-Engine Systems

Niagara · Blueprint · UE5 · Mocap Data
Mocap data
as particle driver.

Particle swarms needed organic motion quality only real mocap could produce — procedural noise wasn't enough. At the required scale, particle count caused frame drops on target hardware.

Mapped mocap data as a Niagara positional driver, built Blueprint runtime controls, then GPU profiled to identify overdraw bottlenecks and consolidated emitters to hit frame-rate targets.

→ Stable frame-rate · Encore Creative Festival, Ohio 2025
