Production Problem

The project needed usable animation data that could move from a live performer into a real-time character workflow. Raw capture is not production-ready by itself, so the key challenge was preserving performance intention while solving calibration, cleanup, skeleton mismatch, scale, feet, and retargeting issues.

Ideation / TD Approach

I treated mocap as a delivery pipeline rather than only a recording session. Every take had to be named, tracked, cleaned, retargeted, validated against the character, and handed off in a form that directors and artists could review without losing time to technical file problems.

Workflow

Step 01

Capture Prep

Prepared the capture setup, performer calibration, take naming, movement requirements, and review expectations before recording.

Step 02

On-Set Capture

Captured performance data while tracking usable takes, marker / tracking issues, and notes for cleanup.

Step 03

Cleanup

Cleaned capture data and corrected common motion problems before sending it into the target rig workflow.

Step 04

Retargeting

Retargeted motion onto the target character rig and checked skeleton scale, joints, feet, contact, and body weight.

Step 05

Validation & Delivery

Prepared animation outputs for real-time character integration and artist review.

My TD Responsibilities

Capture WorkflowManaged the path from performer to usable animation asset, including naming and take organization.
Technical CleanupSolved motion-data issues before delivery so artists would not inherit broken files.
Retarget ValidationChecked body scale, joint behavior, feet contact, and performance readability on the target rig.
Artist SupportPrepared outputs so directors and artists could focus on performance review rather than data troubleshooting.
Real-Time HandoffDelivered animation assets ready for real-time character integration.
