AI CREATURE / PROCEDURAL ANIMATION / MAYA

Quiet Guardian.

A creature that reads how players move together and responds through its body. Proximity, disturbance and Wind Resonance turn nonverbal co-op behavior into approach, trust, retreat and guidance.

ROLEGameplay Programmer / Technical ArtistENGINEUnity C# · Animation Rigging · Maya
01 / DESIGN PROBLEM

What the feature must solve.

An expressive creature should not be a decorative idle animation or a scripted reward. Its movement must follow legible gameplay rules, respond without text prompts and retain physical believability across different encounter layouts.

02 / TECHNICAL DESIGN

Feature architecture.

The final case should show both designer intent and the underlying code/editor system at a reviewable level.

Creature behavior model

Idle → Observe → Approach → Guide → Retreat. Inputs include player/seed distance, gust change, local disturbance and stable resonance. Hysteresis and cooldown prevent rapid state flicker.

Intent before animation

The creature selects movement intent, target location and attention target in C#. Animation states and procedural layers interpret the same intent; presentation never decides gameplay outcomes.

Maya character pipeline

Production-authored topology, skeleton, skinning and gait clips are prepared and exported with stable transform conventions. Character proportions are considered when tuning look-at and foot placement.

Procedural performance

Unity Animation Rigging blends head/neck gaze, body turn, terrain-aligned feet and reaction layers over authored idle/walk cycles. A clear override policy preserves silhouette and avoids foot sliding.

Player-readable feedback

Pause, step-back, gaze exchange and retreat indicate how the creature reads the shared wind. Audio, particles and camera composition can reinforce intent without turning the encounter into a status UI.

Playable encounter design

A calm approach reveals a safe wind corridor. A forceful approach causes retreat and requires players to adjust behavior. Designers tune trust/discomfort thresholds through Encounter Studio.

Player motion + shared wind→C# behavior state→Steering / navigation→Animator + IK→Feedback + level outcome
03 / REQUIRED MEDIA AND RESULTS

What this completed project must show.

These are deliverables for the intended final portfolio, not fabricated current screenshots, measurements or release history.

Behavior comparison

Record two different player approaches producing recognizable creature decisions in a single scene.

Rig / animation evidence

Maya skeleton and skinning, Unity Animator, head-look rig and ground-contact debug views.

Game-design rationale

Show how discovering the creature's preferences changes players' cooperation rather than rewarding brute-force actions.

Stability tests

Interrupt animations, reverse directions, run on uneven ground, lose player tracking and reset the encounter.
