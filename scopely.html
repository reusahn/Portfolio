AI + BUILD PRACTICE

AI is useful when it shortens the distance between an idea and a test.

I use generative tools for exploration, alternatives, and acceleration, then move the selected direction into deterministic code, 3D tools, or engine systems where behavior and quality can be controlled.

01 / AIGenerate options, not authority

ComfyUI, diffusion, and LLM-assisted ideation help branch possibilities quickly. Selection, rejection, and final creative decisions remain authored.

02 / ENGINEERINGTurn decisions into systems

C++, C#, Python, HLSL, Blueprints, Unity and Unreal connect mechanics, runtime state, graphics, inputs, data flows, and tools.

03 / ARTProduction-level visual judgment

Real-time lighting, materials, digital humans, animation, shaders, composition, and DCC workflows let me cross the boundary between implementation and visual direction.

04 / ITERATIONTest where the work is experienced

Director review, headset testing, public installations, and user-facing deployments have taught me to revise from observed behavior rather than assumptions.

What I am not claiming: I have not run a large-scale live mobile game economy or a mature player analytics program. My direct evidence is rapid prototyping, deployed interactive systems, headset and audience testing, creative feedback loops, and AI-assisted production. I would bring that builder mindset into Scopely's live player learning loop.
