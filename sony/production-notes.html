Start with the receiving representation.

Identity sources, performance streams, character rigs, and deforming surface caches serve different stages. Establish the required destination before converting the capture package.

ProductionSource and challengeDecision / delivered representation
Shininho ↗A functional Unity prototype lost likeness; separate capture streams required editing.Rebuild from high-resolution source through Wrap / MetaHuman; clean, reconstruct, retarget, and assemble body / face performance in Unreal.
Row ↗Ten different source packages, missing editorial beats, and competing head motion.Specify intake and fallback requirements; develop each identity, rebuild timing, and align facial head motion with the body rig.
Gang Se-hwang ↗A portrait-derived face needed capture-compatible expressions and believable clothing.Custom facial targets and a debugged coefficient interface; card hair and synchronized garment Alembic in Unity.
When It Seen ↗The receiving simulation team needed moving body and facial surfaces.Synchronize per performer, then deliver full-character Alembic geometry for cloth-contact work.

Keep the handoff contract explicit.

At intake and export, record subject / take identity, frame range, rate, timing convention, units, up axis, included meshes, and the receiving application. Reopen the delivered asset and review the actual deformation and timing in its destination.

Checks declared metadata and source-file presence, calculates common-event offsets, and produces JSON / HTML findings. Source code and a synthetic example are available; the prototype does not decode media formats or judge animation quality.
