01 / capture sources

Preserve the body and facial streams as separate production assets.

MOVIN TRACIN supplied body motion. An iPhone on a Rokoko head rig supplied the facial-performance workflow through Live Link Face / MetaHuman Animator.

The three dancers’ large physical actions and facial expressions needed independent review before integration.

Production decision

Keep source streams independently reviewable until the character performance is assembled.

02 / body face synchronization

Align each performer’s body and face in Unreal.

I manually aligned the streams for each dancer and reviewed the integrated body and facial motion as an individual performance.

The synchronized result became the source for geometry-cache delivery.

Production decision

Review each performer’s timing before baking the less editable handoff representation.

03 / simulation handoff

Deliver the moving surface required for cloth contact.

I exported synchronized body and facial deformation as full-character Alembic geometry, without clothing.

The downstream Houdini team used that moving surface for cloth-contact and imprint simulation. The video shows their downstream result using the delivered geometry.

Production decision

Deliver the geometry needed by the receiving department, with its contribution clearly credited.
