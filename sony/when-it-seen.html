Three dancers · Cross-DCC handoff

When It Seen

Three dancers’ separately captured body and facial performances were synchronized in Unreal and exported as full-character Alembic geometry for downstream cloth-contact simulation.

RoleMotion Capture Specialist
Period2026
ProductionThree dancers · Cross-DCC handoff
ToolsMOVIN TRACIN · iPhone · Unreal · Alembic

My contribution

I integrated body and facial performance, manually synchronized the streams per dancer in Unreal, and delivered deforming body-and-face geometry as Alembic caches.

Production context

The downstream team performed the Houdini cloth-contact and imprint simulation. My responsibility ended at the synchronized, simulation-ready character geometry.

01 / Preserve body and face as separate production assets02 / Synchronize the performance before baking geometry03 / Deliver the representation the next department needs
01 / Production decision

Preserve body and face as separate production assets.

MOVIN TRACIN provided body motion, while an iPhone on a Rokoko head rig supplied facial capture through the Live Link Face / MetaHuman Animator workflow. The dancers’ forceful movement and expressive faces needed independent review and timing control.

Why this approach

Retain separate streams until the performances have been evaluated and synchronized per dancer.

02 / Production decision

Synchronize the performance before baking geometry.

I manually aligned body and face for each performer in Unreal. A fixed offset copied between performers would not establish a reliable integrated result, so each dancer received an individual timing pass.

Why this approach

Review the animated body and face together before converting them into a less editable delivery format.

Production reasoning

Review the animated body and face together before converting them into a less editable delivery format.

03 / Production decision

Deliver the representation the next department needs.

Houdini cloth-contact work required the moving body and facial surface. I exported full-character Alembic caches, without clothing, so the downstream team could use the deformations directly for contact and imprint simulation.

Why this approach

A skeleton-only export would not provide the required simulation surface; deliver synchronized geometry.

Production reasoning

A skeleton-only export would not provide the required simulation surface; deliver synchronized geometry.
