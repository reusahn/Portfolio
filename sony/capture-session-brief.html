Say it clearly on stage.

Example direction / Template

“We will record a short neutral pose, then the action. Begin after the shared cue and return to a comfortable resting position at the end. I will review the take before we repeat it. If you need a break or want to stop, tell me and we will pause.”

“The last take lost tracking during the floor movement. We will keep your action and repeat that section. I will check the capture volume and marker visibility before we start.”
