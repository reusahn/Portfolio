Planned independent study / Acquisition and results to be produced

3D / 4D
Capture Validation

A production study designed to connect performer communication, acquisition choices, reconstruction quality, temporal behavior, and final delivery.

The study design and review protocol below are complete. This page does not represent a recorded capture session or completed 4D reconstruction.

01 / Acquire

Define the subject and the signal.

Capture plan, participant instructions, camera / depth settings, scale reference, neutral and expressive takes, common timing event.

02 / Reconstruct & compare

Separate likeness from temporal behavior.

Document the representation and reconstruction setup. Compare controlled views and matching performance ranges.

03 / Deliver & inspect

Check the destination.

Document export format, frame range, rate, scale, playback hardware, and the receiving application’s result.

Research question.

Which acquisition and representation choices preserve identity and temporal coherence well enough for a short performance to remain usable during character development and final visualization?

Capture plan.

PassRequired sourceControlled or recorded factors
Identity / static referenceNeutral front, three-quarter, profile and surface reference; supported depth or multi-view sourceSubject position, lighting, focal length / field of view, capture distance, exposure, scale reference and source resolution
Short performanceA repeatable expression and motion passage, common sync cue, reference video and declared capture representationFrame rate, timing convention, movement range, occlusion, duration and capture-system limitations
Temporal representationDocumented reconstruction or supplied 4D source, exported sequence, stable frame-range specificationRepresentation, processing settings, topology behavior, missing frames and deformation / surface artifacts

Compare distinct representations.

A rigged MetaHuman driven by performance coefficients and a time-varying reconstructed surface are distinct outputs. The study will document each representation on its own terms and avoid presenting skeletal animation as a completed 4D-capture reconstruction.

Rigged character route

Review likeness transfer, expression correspondence, body / face alignment, deformation quality, and editability. Capture and manual corrections must remain identifiable.

Temporal reconstruction route

Review silhouette, surface / texture continuity, occlusion failures, frame completeness, representation limits, and the actual playback requirements.

Evaluation protocol.

QuestionEvidence to produceReport without invented results
Does the person remain recognizable?Source / output comparison in matched camera views and lightingDescribe changes, visible failure regions, and the limits of the reference material
Is timing coherent?Reference and output with matching frame counters and visible sync eventMeasured offset, source duration, output duration and any resampling assumptions
Is the output stable over time?A fixed passage containing facial movement and occlusionMissing frames and localized surface / texture / deformation failures; define how each is counted
Can the next application use it?Actual import and playback readbackFormat, units, frame range, frame rate, file size, hardware and observed playback or render cost
Why choose one production route?Decision record linked to the same controlled passageTrade-offs in likeness, temporal artifacts, editability and delivery cost

Final evidence sequence.

Each recorded sequence must identify the actual operator, source, representation, settings, and measured findings. A planned demonstration is not a historical production result.

Definition of completion.

The study becomes a completed case after the capture session, declared reconstruction route, matched comparisons, measured delivery record, and receiving-application checks are present. The acquisition, representation, and correction contributions must be attributable.

My ongoing Interactive Digital Humans research provides a separate route into Gaussian-human control and representation questions. Its research direction is not treated as evidence that this capture study has already been performed.
