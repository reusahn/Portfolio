Character variants · Material transitions

Age-Variant Digital Human

One depth-video identity supported a child-body adaptation and adult age states, using structural changes where proportions mattered and material variations where surface appearance was sufficient.

RoleTechnical Artist
Period2024
ProductionCharacter variants · Material transitions
ToolsMetaHuman Identity · CC4 · Unity / Unreal

My contribution

I processed the facial source through MetaHuman Identity, integrated a CC4 child body, developed adult age-state surfaces, and prepared smooth transitions between material variants.

Production context

This was a digital-human collaboration with Yaloo. Middle-aged and elderly states reused the adult geometry with material and texture changes; they were not separate reconstructed characters.

01 / Establish one identity before deriving variants02 / Change the body where the silhouette needs to change
01 / Production decision

Establish one identity before deriving variants.

The depth-video capture became the base MetaHuman Identity. Building a common adult character first provided a consistent facial reference for all later age states.

Why this approach

Keep the identity reference stable while separating proportion changes from surface changes.

02 / Production decision

Change the body where the silhouette needs to change.

The younger version paired the identity-bearing MetaHuman head with a CC4 child body. Middle-aged and elderly versions instead used material and texture variations on the existing adult geometry.

Why this approach

Use a structural solution for body proportions and a surface solution for the adult age treatments.
