01 / identity intake

Standardize the source package, then validate each person.

I specified separate identity footage, head-turn and teeth-visible references, and a singing take recorded to a shared metronome. The artist recorded the participants, and I processed all ten MetaHuman identities.

When a Live Link source could not be used reliably, supplemental Polycam head data provided a recovery path. Each generated identity was checked individually against its reference footage.

Production decision

Use one intake specification and a per-person quality review.

02 / ensemble look development

Balance skin and groom response across five overlapping faces.

Shared lighting did not produce equally readable skin for every participant. I tuned material roughness and key-light contribution per character, then adjusted eyes, teeth, groom response, and bright-hair density.

The final composition grouped ten characters into two ensembles of five. Close-up faces remained at LOD 0, with groom shadow and quality settings adjusted for the overlap; unnecessary groom simulation was disabled.

Production decision

Retain each participant’s identity while validating the shared composition.

03 / facial editorial

Build the performance coverage the installation needs.

Sequencer organized the canon entrances, facial performances, parent-actor reveals, view changes, and exits. I rebuilt missing introduction, pause, and ending beats from the captured material.

For singing-to-idle sections, I removed mouth-performance keys while retaining useful blinking and subtle head motion, then applied selective smoothing and key reduction.

Production decision

Create editable performance coverage rather than leaving gaps between recorded phrases.

04 / head motion stability

Make the facial and body systems agree on head movement.

The facial recording carried head movement as well as facial deformation. Mismatched head motion between that performance and the body-side setup produced visible vibration.

I transferred the relevant head / IK motion into the body Control Rig so the body and facial layers followed the same source, then aligned head motion, facial animation, audio, and reveals in the canon.

Production decision

Resolve the competing head-motion representations before final timing review.

05 / installation delivery

Re-light for the presentation and deliver two installation channels.

Morning, sunset, and night treatments used different HDRIs and character-lighting decisions. The night version separated character alpha and background before compositing in Premiere.

The final output was a looping two-channel presentation for two square panels, incorporating front and side views of the character ensembles.

Production decision

Validate the intended display framing and lighting variants as part of the deliverable.
