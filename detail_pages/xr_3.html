VR Experience · The Cave Seoul · 2023

Be NARCI

Year 2023
Type VR Immersive Experience
Venue The Cave Seoul
Companion NARCI (2023)
Role Director · Technical Artist
XR / INTERACTIVE PRODUCTIONVR / ROLE REVERSAL

What happens when the viewer holds the power?

Be NARCI reverses the perspective of NARCI. Instead of enduring another character's control, the VR participant can change free-moving human-like figures into rigid statues or metallic remains.

Player experience

The participant acts on the figures and witnesses the transformation. An encounter with manipulated bodies becomes a question about control, self-validation, and consequence.

My build and direction

I directed and technically developed the companion VR experience, shaping the transformation behavior and the relationship between a viewer's choice and the virtual figures' state.

Interaction design

The key shift is agency. Reversing who can act on whom allows the same theme to be experienced from a different position, using visual transformation in place of a didactic explanation.
