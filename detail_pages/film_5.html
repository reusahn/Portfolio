3D Short Film

Mythomania

Year 2024
Type 3D Animated Short
Runtime 4 min
Role Director

Three strangers awaken on a drifting raft, with no memory of how they got there. As they share mysterious meat to survive, silence unravels their darkest guilt — and those who try to justify their past begin to vanish, one by one.

Mythomania explores the psychology of self-deception and the weight of unspoken guilt. Set on a minimalist stage — a raft adrift in an infinite sea — the film uses 3D animation to construct a world where the boundaries between reality and delusion dissolve alongside its characters.
