3D Short Film

Mythomania

Year 2024
Type 3D Animated Short
Runtime 4 min
Role Director
FILM / DIRECTORIAL CASE STUDY3D ANIMATED FILM / DIRECTION

Three strangers, one raft, no reliable account of the past.

Mythomania is a short 3D animation in which strangers wake on a raft and confront their own accounts of guilt and survival. The infinite sea and enclosed platform turn self-deception into a spatial condition.

On screen

The audience watches characters negotiate, rationalize, and disappear within a deliberately sparse environment. The world is not realistic geography; it is a stage for conflicting stories.

Direction / production

I directed the animated short, using 3D staging, a restricted setting, character timing, and composition to sustain a narrative without extensive environment changes.

Why this form

The raft allowed the film to concentrate production and attention on character relationships. The small world creates narrative pressure while leaving room for a visually controlled 3D atmosphere.
