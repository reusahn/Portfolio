An ancient flute becomes wind, waves, and living light.

The Nature of Manpasikjeok interprets a Korean legend of a flute believed to calm disasters through wind-like motion, wave patterns, and generative imagery. The piece was recognized as a finalist in the Daegu Media Art Competition.

What the viewer experiences

The audience encounters sound and airflow as visual movement rather than a literal retelling. Changing patterns suggest the mythical instrument's influence on an environment.

My role & production

I conceived and directed the generative media piece, connecting the legend's sonic imagery to moving forms, simulation-inspired behavior, and screen composition.

Creative / technical decision

Instead of relying on character narration, the work lets material motion carry the story. The legend's breath becomes the organizing principle for the image.
