Conversation becomes part of the way out.

Escape Metro is a Unity VR escape game set inside an uncanny subway. Players explore, solve puzzles, and converse with a ChatGPT-powered character as part of navigating an environment with unfamiliar rules.

Player experience

The player is not taken out of the world to use a chatbot. Dialogue happens inside the experience while space, puzzles, and the character's responses contribute to an unfolding escape.

My build and direction

I directed and technically developed the VR experience, integrating an AI conversation layer with Unity interaction, spatial navigation, game progression, and public pop-up presentation.

Interaction design

The design decision was to place conversation inside the escape loop, not as a standalone demonstration of text generation. The case shows integration of a model-backed character, not the invention of a new language model.
