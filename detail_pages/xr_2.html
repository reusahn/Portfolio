VR Experience · The Cave Seoul · 2023

NARCI

Year 2023
Type VR Immersive Experience
Venue The Cave Seoul
Role Director · Technical Artist
XR / INTERACTIVE PRODUCTIONVR / PSYCHOLOGICAL NARRATIVE

A closed elevator becomes a psychological scene.

NARCI is an immersive VR experience placing visitors in an elevator with confronting narcissistic figures. The constrained journey follows emotional manipulation and a gradual attempt to recover one's sense of self.

Player experience

Viewers occupy the enclosed space directly, meet figures at uncomfortable proximity, and experience the repetition and confinement of a conversation they cannot simply walk away from.

My build and direction

I conceived and directed the VR piece and developed its real-time presentation as a Technical Artist, shaping spatial staging, character encounters, timing, and the participant's point of view.

Interaction design

The elevator's small size was a deliberate constraint. Rather than present abstract psychological ideas through narration, it creates repeated face-to-face situations through VR presence.
