Single Channel Video · Narrative Media Art · 2024

Seon-a's
Family

Year 2024
Type Single Channel Video · Media Art
Role Director · Technical Artist

Seon-a's Family is a storytelling-based media art piece that explores familial conflict and reconciliation through advanced technologies. By integrating diffusion models, large language models (LLMs), and digital human technologies, Seon-a's journey unfolds across multiple layers of time and space — transcending linear narrative conventions.

Viewers are invited to interactively engage with her life story, embracing new forms of digital storytelling without resistance. Blending emotional depth with technical sophistication, the work creates an immersive experience that reflects on the harmony — and tension — between human relationships and emerging technologies.
