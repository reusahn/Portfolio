A performer assembled from portrait, voice, and response.

AI Choi Jung-hoon reconstructs a pop vocalist as a real-time conversational digital character. The prototype combines a modeled face and body with generated speech, facial motion, and visitor sensing so that the character can answer rather than merely play a prerecorded clip.

What people experience

Visitors speak to the character, hear a synthesized response, watch corresponding facial animation, and experience physical gestures and proximity as part of the encounter.

What I implemented

As Principal Investigator and Technical Director, I assembled the character workflow from Character Creator/ZBrush/Maya into Unity, connecting speech transcription, GPT-based response, ElevenLabs speech output, blendshape lip sync, and Azure Kinect sensing.

Why I designed it this way

I treated voice, performance, timing, and interaction as one product problem. The project tests the perceptual distance between a recognizable likeness and a responsive artificial persona; it is not evidence of autonomous learning by the character.

Technical Pipeline
High-res portrait → Character Creator 4 Headshot → ZBrush refinement → Maya rigging & UV → Unity real-time rendering
Digital Human Construction

High-resolution portrait input processed through Character Creator 4 Headshot, refined in ZBrush, rigged and UV-unwrapped in Maya, then integrated into Unity for real-time rendering.

Character Creator 4 ZBrush Maya Unity
Voice Cloning

Interview and performance samples processed through ElevenLabs Instant Voice Cloning, enabling spontaneous speech generation that mimics tone, color, and cadence. Real-time lip-sync achieved through blendshape animation.

ElevenLabs Blendshape
Conversational AI Integration

GPT-based persona model embedded into Unity. Audience speech → transcription → GPT response → ElevenLabs TTS → real-time facial animation. A coroutine-based pipeline ensures continuous, natural conversational flow.

GPT API TTS Unity
Sensor-Based Interaction

Azure Kinect provides body tracking, gesture detection, and depth information, allowing the AI human to react physically to visitors — leaning, nodding, and mirroring engagement. Audience forms rendered as 3D point clouds.

Azure Kinect Point Cloud

The project explores AI-mediated affection and the co-construction of identity in machine-human systems. By merging machine learning, motion capture, and emotional projection, the digital Choi Jung-hoon becomes both a subject of empathy and a simulation of it.

This work rethinks authorship and digital intimacy in the era of computational performers — questioning what it means to know, love, or lose someone who was never entirely human.
