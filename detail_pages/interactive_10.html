Media Installation · CalArts, 2025

Beads
Wall

Year 2025
Type Generative Media Installation
Venue California Institute of the Arts
Role Director · Technical Artist
AI / INTERACTION CASE STUDYPARTICIPATORY MEDIA / REAL-TIME VISION

A passing body leaves a temporary field of light.

Beads Wall transforms human movement into a shifting, bead-like digital image. Visitors' silhouettes emerge in colored points and dissolve again as people move through the installation.

What people experience

A passerby becomes an unplanned performer: their position and gesture reorganize a wall of color while a physical gel layer gives the display additional depth.

What I implemented

I directed and technically developed the responsive media installation, connecting body-driven visual behavior and large-format display into an accessible encounter.

Why I designed it this way

The response avoids a fixed avatar representation. Instead, recognition is temporary: as the body moves, the image disperses, making participation visible without asking visitors to learn controls.
