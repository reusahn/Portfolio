Media Art · Sound-Reactive Installation · 2022

Fall of
Minds

Year 2022
Type Generative Media Installation
Venues Pangyo Pameus Mall · Samsung Hospital · Incheon Museum
Role Coding Director · Artist

Fall of Minds is an interactive media art installation that visualizes ambient noise as a generative force. The surrounding soundscape becomes a variable — shaping the movement, density, and complexity of a cascading cloud-like waterfall projected in real time.

As the acoustic environment shifts, so does the visual form — quiet moments produce gentle, sparse flows, while louder spaces generate dense, turbulent cascades. The installation turns collective sound into collective image, making the invisible auditory environment of a space suddenly, viscerally visible.
