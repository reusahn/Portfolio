Fall of
Minds

Year 2022
Type Generative Media Installation
Venues Pangyo Pameus Mall · Samsung Hospital · Incheon Museum
Role Coding Director · Artist
MOVING IMAGE / PRODUCTION CASE STUDYAUDIO REACTIVITY / GENERATIVE SYSTEMS

The sound of a place shapes its moving image.

Fall of Minds is a sound-reactive installation in which ambient audio drives a generative waterfall. As the acoustic environment changes, the visual field changes its movement, density, and apparent turbulence.

What the viewer experiences

Visitors see the shared sound of a space translated into a continuous image. No explicit command is needed: ordinary activity around the piece becomes a source of variation.

My role & production

As Coding Director and Artist, I implemented the real-time connection between ambient sound parameters and the waterfall's generative visual behavior for public display.

Creative / technical decision

I used environmental sound rather than a prescribed interaction gesture. This made the installation responsive to a crowd, not just to one participant at a time.
