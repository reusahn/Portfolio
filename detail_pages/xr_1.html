Fineo,
Translated Universe

Year 2019
Type VR Data Visualization Installation
Award Grand Prize — KMU Art & Design Exhibition
Role Director · Technical Artist
Grand Prize — 16th Kookmin University Art & Design Exhibition

Fineo, Translated Universe VR reconstructs the periodic table by mapping elemental data through harmonograph-based functions, generating a new system of visual logic. Integrating constellations and elemental structures, the piece presents a speculative universe — an immersive data visualization where science and imagination converge into a reimagined cosmology.

Each element becomes a node in a vast harmonic field, its atomic properties translated into wave frequencies and orbital paths. The viewer floats through this generated universe, navigating a space where chemistry becomes constellation and data becomes dreamscape.
