VR Game · 2019

Graffiti
Freedom

Year 2019
Type VR Game · Public Space
Tech Unity · VR
Role Director · Technical Artist

Graffiti Freedom is a VR experience that allows players to spray graffiti freely inside a virtual subway station — reclaiming public space without violating real-world laws. By blending street art with immersive technology, the work explores freedom of expression, civic resistance, and the tension between legality and creativity in urban environments.

The subway wall becomes an endlessly available canvas — no arrest, no cleanup, no consequence. Graffiti Freedom asks: what does it mean to own a space? And what happens to the impulse to mark, to sign, to say "I was here" when the wall can never be fully claimed by anyone?
