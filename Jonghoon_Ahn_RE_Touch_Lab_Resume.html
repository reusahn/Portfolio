Current Research

Interactive Digital Humans · Independent Research
4D Humans · Gaussian Avatars · Embodied Interaction

Investigating whether real-time 4D human representations can expose semantically meaningful, locally controllable interaction regions while preserving identity, temporal coherence, and rendering quality. Current stage: baseline reproduction and locality / leakage probes.

SplatStream Lab · 3D Gaussian Splatting Compression / Streaming
Pruning · Quantization · CUDA Rendering · Rate-Distortion

Built a reproducible evaluation pipeline for compact 3D Gaussian representations and progressive transmission experiments.

Verified CUDA experiment, Mip-NeRF 360 Bonsai: 947,033 Gaussians; 185.43 MiB baseline → 70.04 MiB prune25_q16; 2.65× reduction; 27.6234 dB PSNR; 0.900674 SSIM; 0.165871 LPIPS.
Ray-Scene Acceleration
C++17 · BVH · Binned SAH · Uniform Grid · 3D-DDA

Implemented and validated multiple ray-scene traversal structures with correctness tests, emphasizing systems-level graphics implementation.
