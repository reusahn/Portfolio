What I owned

I built the practical digital-human age variants: the MetaHuman identity base, child-body integration, and material-driven middle-aged/elderly transitions.

Middle-aged and elderly states were texture/material changes, not new reconstructed meshes. The child version used a CC4 body combined with the MetaHuman head.

TASK 01 / 03

Depth Video → Identity

Problem
Create a recognizable real-time Yaloo identity quickly.
Decision
Use depth video as the MetaHuman Identity source.
Implementation
Process depth capture through MetaHuman Identity to create base face/mesh.
Result
Practical adult identity base.

TASK 02 / 03

Child Body Adaptation

Problem
The child version needed proportions not provided by the chosen body setup.
Decision
Keep the MetaHuman head; replace body solution.
Implementation
Bring CC4 child body and integrate with MetaHuman head.
Result
Younger variant with more appropriate proportions.

TASK 03 / 03

Material-Based Aging

Problem
Middle-aged and elderly states needed smooth transitions without new geometry.
Decision
Use age-specific textures/materials with fades.
Implementation
Prepare middle/elderly material variants and animate fade in/out between states.
Result
Smooth age transition with minimal geometry complexity.
