Jonghoon Ahn안종훈

Jonghoon Ahn is a media artist and filmmaker working between Seoul and the United States. Across film, animation, interactive media, and digital humans, he examines memory and emotion and the boundary between physical and virtual experience.

He uses AI, motion recognition, real-time 3D, projection, and neural representations to extend moving images beyond the screen and into space and audience experience. He received an MFA in Art and Technology from California Institute of the Arts and is currently pursuing a PhD in Media Arts and Technology at the University of California, Santa Barbara.

2026–ONGOING

Interaction-Ready 4D Humans

This research examines how deformation spreads across semantically meaningful regions in Gaussian human representations. Current experiments reconstruct the pretrained HUGS model and compare learned LBS behavior with SMPL-based controls. Counterfactual ablation is the next step before making a causal claim.

Biography

Jonghoon Ahn / 안종훈

Jonghoon Ahn is a media artist and filmmaker based between Seoul and the United States. Working across film, animation, interactive media, and digital humans, he explores memory and emotion and the boundary between physical and virtual experience.

AI, motion recognition, real-time 3D, projection, and neural representation are treated as artistic media rather than ends in themselves. His work asks how moving images can occupy space, respond to bodies, and change through the presence of an audience. He received an MFA in Art and Technology from California Institute of the Arts and is currently pursuing a PhD in Media Arts and Technology at the University of California, Santa Barbara.
