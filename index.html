Interaction-Ready 4D Humans

Using Apple's pretrained HUGS baseline on NeuMan Seattle, I built diagnostic tooling for SMPL/LBS semantics, Gaussian locality, and learned deformation.

Biography

Jonghoon Ahn is a media artist and filmmaker working between Seoul and the United States.

Using AI, motion sensing, real-time 3D, projection, and performance capture, he experiments with how moving images can extend beyond the screen into space, participation, and embodied experience.
