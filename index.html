Introduce

Jonghoon Ahn
New Media Artist & Film Director

Jonghoon is a new media artist and film director who combines film and interactive XR elements to explore the boundary between reality and virtuality. His experimental films and XR engage audiences through participation and technology, often addressing themes of social inequality.

portfolio

AI Human


1. Choi Jung Hoon AI Human
- Research Project in California Institute of the Arts

VIDEO
잔나비 밴드의 보컬 최정훈의 데이터로 제작한 AI Human. LLM 모델에 목소리, 성격, 외형, 인적사항을 대입한 뒤 최정훈 외형의 디지털 휴먼에 융합하여 관객과 마이크를 통해 소통한다.
An AI human is created using data from Choi Jung-hoon, the vocalist of the band Jannabi. After integrating his voice, personality, appearance, and personal information into a language model (LLM), it transforms into a digital human with Choi Jung-hoon's appearance and communicates with the audience through a microphone.



2. Seon-ah's Family
- Liveaction and 3D Animation, 8min, 2025

VIDEO
‘선아의 가족’은 선아라는 AI 가상인간의 가족 이야기를 영화로 풀어내고, 라이브액션과 함께 가상인간 선아와 상호작용할 수 있는 인터랙티브 미디어 아트가 동시에 진행된다.
'Seon-ah's Family' tells the story of the family of an AI virtual human named Seon-ah through a movie. In addition to live-action footage, interactive media art allows users to interact with the virtual human Seon-ah simultaneously.

1. To Eternity
- Interactive Media Art, Yakangjeondang, National Asia Culture Center, 2020

VIDEO
1948년 5월 18일에 일어났던 민주항쟁 운동을 촬영했던 사진들이 관객의 사진 위로 실시간 모자이크화 된다. 관객의 헌화하는 자세를 인식하고 사진이 완성되는 광주민주화운동을 추모하는 디지털 헌화대 작품.
Photos taken during the democratic uprising on May 18, 1948, are transformed into a real-time mosaic overlaying the audience's own photos. Additionally, a digital wreath stand commemorates the Gwangju Democratization Movement by recognizing the audience's wreath-laying gestures and completing the photograph.

1. Non-Done Record
- Short Film, 8min 40sec, 2024

2024 Semi Finalist, Flickers’ Rhode Island Film Festival, US
2024 Finalist, Sundance Ignite X Adobe Fellowship, US
2024 Silver Award, Independent Shorts Awards in Jan, US
2024 Award of Merit, Accolade Global Film Festival , US

영화감독을 꿈꾸는 청년, 상대적으로 자신보다 우월한 사람을 만나게 되며 꿈을 위해 다시 노력한다. 하지만 상대적인 박탈감을 느끼고 자신의 인생을 스스로 마감하고 만다. 죽어가는 그의 의식들이 영화로 완성되어 무의미한 공간에서 미완성의 기록들이 되풀이 된다.
A young man aspiring to become a film director meets someone who is relatively superior to him and redoubles his efforts to achieve his dream. However, he feels a sense of relative deprivation and decides to end his own life. His fading consciousness is transformed into a film, and incomplete records endlessly repeat themselves in a meaningless space.
