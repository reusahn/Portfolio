Jonghoon Ahn안종훈

Jonghoon Ahn is a media artist and filmmaker working across film, animation, interactive media, digital humans, and real-time graphics. His practice explores memory and emotion, and the shifting boundary between physical and virtual experience.

Using AI, motion sensing, real-time 3D, projection, and neural representations, he investigates how moving images can extend beyond the screen into space, bodies, and audience experience. He holds an MFA in Art and Technology from California Institute of the Arts and is currently a PhD student in Media Arts and Technology at the University of California, Santa Barbara.

Research

Current research in digital humans and neural graphics

Research is presented here as part of the artistic and technical practice, with claims kept separate from completed artworks.

2026–ONGOING

Interaction-Ready 4D Humans

A study of semantic locality and learned deformation in Gaussian human representations. Current experiments reconstruct the pretrained HUGS checkpoint and compare learned LBS behavior against SMPL-derived controls. The present result is mechanism-level evidence, with causal ablation planned next.

Biography

Jonghoon Ahn / 안종훈

Jonghoon Ahn is a media artist and filmmaker based between Seoul and the United States. Working across film, animation, interactive media, and digital humans, he explores human memory and emotion and the boundary between physical and virtual experience.

Through AI, motion recognition, real-time 3D, projection, and emerging neural representations, he experiments with ways moving images can extend beyond the screen into spatial and embodied audience experience. He received an MFA in Art and Technology from California Institute of the Arts and is currently pursuing a PhD in Media Arts and Technology at the University of California, Santa Barbara.
