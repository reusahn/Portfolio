Research

Research into interactive human representations

Practice-based questions become technical experiments: human deformation, interactive 4D representation, embodied perception and neural graphics. Research in progress is identified separately from completed work.

2026–ONGOING

Interaction-Ready 4D Humans

This research examines how deformation spreads across semantically meaningful regions in Gaussian human representations. Current experiments reconstruct the pretrained HUGS model and compare learned LBS behavior with SMPL-based controls. Counterfactual ablation is the next step before making a causal claim.

Neural Graphics & Video Algorithms

Biography

Jonghoon Ahn / 안종훈

I am a technical artist, technical director, and filmmaker who makes ideas operational. I have built Unity/Unreal experiences with AI characters, real-time animation, computer vision, virtual humans, and interactive graphics, from early technical decisions to public exhibition and delivery.

I developed C# / HLSL runtime graphics for an independent game directed by Erick Oh and work on digital-human and performance systems with media artist Yaloo. Museum commissions have involved visitors, sensors, software, screens, and hardware operating together in public. Alongside those systems, I direct films and experiment with generated imagery, where cinematic decisions shape how new technology is felt and understood.

My ongoing UCSB Media Arts & Technology PhD research investigates controllable 4D human representations, semantic deformation, and the relation between virtual-human fidelity and social perception. Earlier, I completed an MFA in Art & Technology at CalArts. Research questions, production engineering, and cinematic storytelling are connected parts of the same practice.
