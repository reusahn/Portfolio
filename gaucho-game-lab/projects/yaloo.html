What I contributed

Character continuity across age

Maintained recognizable identity while adapting proportions, surface qualities, hair, and overall character presentation across multiple life stages. The work required balancing visual continuity with technical differences between individual character assets.

Real-time implementation

Integrated character assets into Unity and Unreal, tuned materials and lighting, connected animation content, and supported repeated iteration for exhibition and cinematic use.

Production troubleshooting

Resolved issues that crossed DCC and engine boundaries, including skin, hair, material, animation, and presentation problems that would otherwise surface late in delivery.

Long-term collaboration

Worked as an ongoing technical partner rather than a one-off asset vendor, supporting changing creative requirements across multiple outputs and versions.

For Gaucho Game Lab, this work demonstrates the ability to support character-heavy productions over time, adapt to changing art direction, and bridge visual goals with engine constraints.
