Pipeline and technical contribution

01 · Identity and reconstruction

Built the character pipeline from source scans into production-ready digital humans, resolving topology, wrap, eye, hair, and surface issues before engine integration.

02 · Body and face capture

Integrated OptiTrack body capture with facial performance, supplemented missing hand or facial information where needed, and kept body/face timing synchronized.

03 · Cleanup and retargeting

Used MotionBuilder and Maya for cleanup, HumanIK, retargeting, timing correction, and animation validation before final Unreal assembly.

04 · Unreal assembly

Set up body and face animation in Unreal, baked and validated sequences, assembled performances in Sequencer, and handled lighting and real-time presentation.

05 · Real-time effects

Used Niagara and engine-side look development to support the final visual language without breaking character readability or performance.

06 · VR restaging

Adapted the work for VR, checking scale, viewpoint, character readability, lighting, and presentation in an immersive environment.

Why this matters for Gaucho Game Lab: this project demonstrates production ownership across DCC tools, capture systems, animation, character look development, and Unreal runtime integration rather than a single isolated asset task.

Technical problems solved

  • Scan-derived geometry and MetaHuman compatibility
  • Body/face synchronization and timing cleanup
  • Retargeting between capture and runtime skeletons
  • Eye and hair correction for believable real-time characters
  • Sequencer validation and VR presentation

Game-team contribution

The same pipeline knowledge transfers directly to character implementation, mocap cleanup, retargeting, facial systems, animation integration, technical troubleshooting, and interdisciplinary production support.
