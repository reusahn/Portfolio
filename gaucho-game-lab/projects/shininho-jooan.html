End-to-end character and performance pipeline

01 · Source preparation

Prepared scan-derived character material and resolved geometry, topology, surface, eye, and hair issues that would become more visible after MetaHuman reconstruction.

02 · MetaHuman reconstruction

Translated the real-person source into a production-ready MetaHuman-based identity while preserving recognizable proportions and a stable engine-ready character structure.

03 · Body capture

Captured body performance with OptiTrack / Motive and organized the source animation so each take could be evaluated independently before integration.

04 · Face and supplemental data

Integrated facial performance and supplemental hand information where the original body stream did not carry enough expressive detail.

05 · Cleanup in MotionBuilder / Maya

Used MotionBuilder and Maya to repair solved motion, remove visible capture artifacts, correct timing, and prepare the data for stable retargeting.

06 · HumanIK and retargeting

Mapped the cleaned performance through HumanIK and Unreal retargeting, checking joint behavior and deformation rather than assuming the capture skeleton would transfer directly.

07 · Body / face synchronization

Aligned body and facial performances, baked the required tracks, and validated the combined performance as one character rather than separate animation layers.

08 · Unreal assembly

Assembled the character, animation, lighting, and shot structure in Sequencer and corrected issues that only became visible in final real-time presentation.

09 · Real-time VFX

Added Niagara-based effects and engine-side look development while keeping character silhouette, facial readability, and timing intact.

10 · VR re-staging

Reworked scale, gaze, viewpoint, lighting, and scene presentation for immersive viewing, validating the result in VR instead of treating the cinematic camera as the final reference.

Problems solved

  • Scan-derived geometry and MetaHuman compatibility
  • Eye and hair correction for real-time characters
  • Capture cleanup before retargeting
  • Body / face synchronization and baking
  • Retargeting between capture and runtime skeletons
  • Sequencer-level validation after animation integration
  • VR scale, gaze, and spatial presentation

Why this is a game-production case

The work crosses character art, technical animation, capture, runtime integration, VFX, lighting, and immersive validation. The core skill is not one isolated asset operation but maintaining a reliable pipeline while the character repeatedly moves between DCC tools and Unreal.
