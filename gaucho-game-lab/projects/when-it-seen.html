When It Seen

Motion-capture and technical-animation work for three dancer actors, combining MOVIN TRACIN body capture with Rokoko and iPhone facial performance, synchronized in Unreal and delivered as full-character Alembic deformation caches.

Motion Capture SpecialistUnreal EngineMOVIN TRACINRokokoiPhone Facial CaptureAlembic

Technical contribution

01 · Multi-performer capture

Managed body-capture material for three dancer actors using MOVIN TRACIN and prepared data for downstream character integration.

02 · Facial capture

Combined Rokoko and iPhone-based facial performance with body motion to preserve performer-specific expression and timing.

03 · Synchronization

Matched body and facial takes per performer and resolved timing mismatches before final character deformation delivery.

04 · Unreal validation

Used Unreal as the integration and validation environment for full-character performance before cache export.

05 · Alembic delivery

Delivered complete character deformation as Alembic caches for downstream Houdini work.

06 · Collaboration boundary

Houdini cloth work was handled by another collaborator and is not claimed here as my contribution.

The value for game production is the same: capture cleanup, body/face synchronization, runtime validation, and clean handoff between animation, engine, and downstream departments.
