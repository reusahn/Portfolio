VR GAME · PUBLIC SPACE INTERACTION2019

Graffiti Freedom

A Unity VR experience that lets players spray graffiti freely inside a virtual subway station, using the simulation of a prohibited real-world action as the core interaction.

Role Director / Technical ArtistStack Unity · VR
Graffiti Freedom16:9 · autoplay · timeline + volume
OVERVIEW

A simple interaction became a way to test presence, agency, and virtual public space.

The project used VR to create a space where the player could mark surfaces without the legal and physical consequences of real-world graffiti. The value was not complexity for its own sake, but the immediacy of performing the action in a spatial environment.

Making the virtual wall respond as a usable surface

The design centered on giving the viewer a direct action—marking public space—rather than asking them only to observe an environment.

Turn a familiar prohibited action into a safe virtual interaction.

Result

The project is useful evidence of the continuity of my Unity/VR practice from 2019 onward.

TAKEAWAY

Graffiti Freedom is intentionally simple, but it marks the start of a long-running focus: building real-time systems where the user's action changes how a 3D space is understood.
