Yaloo Digital Human Pipeline

Built a repeatable digital-human pipeline for age and likeness variation, including child and elderly versions of Yaloo and a digital reconstruction of Yaloo’s grandmother.

Technical Artist / Digital Human TD · Los Angeles & Seoul

Production Problem

The project required multiple versions of a single identity across age, memory, and family resemblance. The production challenge was not only making one believable digital human, but keeping likeness, facial behavior, material response, and real-time performance consistent across several character variants and exhibition venues.

Ideation / TD Approach

I treated the project as a reusable character-pipeline problem. Each variant followed the same logic: define likeness targets, prepare the character asset, test facial response, integrate into UE5, check materials and playback, then package a build that artists could review without touching the technical setup.

Workflow

Step 01

Reference & Likeness Targets

Organized age references, family resemblance cues, and artist notes into clear technical targets for each character variant.

Step 02

Character Asset Prep

Prepared each variant for facial setup, deformation testing, material passes, and real-time engine integration.

Step 03

Facial System & Blendshape Tests

Set up expression support and checked whether subtle performance changes preserved likeness across child, elderly, and grandmother versions.

Step 04

UE5 Integration

Integrated real-time-ready characters into UE5, checked materials, playback, scale, and scene behavior for exhibition presentation.

Step 05

Debugging & Handoff

Troubleshot asset issues, prepared delivery builds, and supported production handoff for Songeun Art Award, Gyeonggi MoMA, and West Seoul Museum of Art.

My TD Responsibilities

Pipeline StructureCreated a repeatable workflow for age and likeness variation instead of solving each character as a one-off asset.
Character SetupPrepared facial setup, blendshape response, material checks, and UE5-ready character delivery.
Artist SupportTranslated artist feedback about likeness, age, and presence into concrete technical changes.
Debugging / QAChecked deformation, materials, playback stability, and venue delivery issues before handoff.
Production DeliverySupported real-time exhibition builds across multiple venues and project iterations.
