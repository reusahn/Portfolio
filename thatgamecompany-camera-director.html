CAMERA FEATURE / LEVEL EDITOR / CINEMACHINE

Adaptive Camera Director.

A cooperative camera system that follows player intent rather than a single target. It preserves shared context, reveals critical paths, and gives level designers reusable framing controls without duplicating Cinemachine.

ROLETechnical Game Designer / Gameplay SystemsENGINEUnity C# · Cinemachine · Custom Editor
01 / DESIGN PROBLEM

What the feature must solve.

One tracking rig cannot serve every moment in a cooperative game. Two players may move apart while the shared seed falls toward a gate; a reactive creature may approach; the camera must communicate the most important action and maintain visibility without stealing control.

02 / TECHNICAL DESIGN

Feature architecture.

The final case should show both designer intent and the underlying code/editor system at a reviewable level.

Gameplay-aware shot arbitration

A priority-based director reads shared seed velocity, two-player world-space positions, encounter transitions, and a creature's interest state. It requests a camera profile without introducing separate physics or gameplay authority.

Composition with Cinemachine

Cinemachine handles target grouping, damping, lens settings and blends. The feature adds game-specific focus intent: seed urgency, shared visibility, reveal time and return-to-player rules.

Occlusion and accessibility

Line-of-sight checks detect blocked seed/guardian targets. Safe framing, target visibility and camera transition limits reduce hidden gameplay cues and avoid abrupt disorienting changes.

Reusable authored camera zones

A ScriptableObject shot profile defines target weights, framing margin, anticipation, transition timing and audio/VFX cues. Level scene handles visualize boundaries and focus targets.

Editor preview & test

A designer scrubs encounter phases, previews two-player separation, compares identical input replay with camera profiles, and saves reusable presets for future levels.

Network-safe presentation

The camera runs locally, reading authoritative replicated world state but never changing it. Different clients may frame the same event differently without affecting game rules.

Gameplay state→Shot intent evaluation→Cinemachine rig / blends→Occlusion safety→Player-facing camera
