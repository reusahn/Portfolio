UNITY VR · PSYCHOLOGICAL EXPERIENCE · 2023

NARCI

A Unity VR work set inside an elevator, where encounters with narcissistic figures progressively shape the viewer’s psychological experience.

Role Director / Interaction DesignerEngine UnityMedium VR
01 / DESIGN PREMISE

Turning psychological pressure into spatial experience.

NARCI places the viewer inside an elevator and stages encounters with narcissistic figures so the emotional premise is experienced through presence and sequence rather than explained only through dialogue.

SpaceElevator as bounded level

A compact environment concentrates attention and makes each encounter feel unavoidable.

ExperienceProgressive psychological change

The work is structured around encounters that gradually erode the viewer's sense of confidence.

MediumUnity VR

The viewer is placed inside the designed situation rather than watching it from outside.

02 / WHY IT MATTERS

Interaction-design evidence for a product team building movement-driven play.

The useful evidence for Nex is rapid Unity prototyping, player-facing spatial feedback, and iterative testing of an embodied interactive experience.
