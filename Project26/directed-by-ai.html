Directed By AI

A film and media installation co-directed with an AI system, using personal facial data to reconstruct virtual family members and combine generative visuals with narrative development.

Role Director / Technical ArtistVenue California Institute of the ArtsTools Diffusion · LLM · Unreal
01 / AI AS COLLABORATOR

Visual character generation and narrative development inside one project.

Directed By AI reconstructs virtual family members from personal facial data. Diffusion models expand the characters’ visual forms, while large language models are used to refine dialogue and narrative flow.

VisualDiffusion models

Used to design and expand the appearance of virtual family characters.

NarrativeLarge language models

Used to support dialogue and story development rather than replace authorship.

DeliveryFilm + media installation

Generated portraits, altered voices, and virtual-character presentation form one integrated work.

02 / HYBRID PRACTICE

AI output still has to enter a production environment.

The project sits alongside my 3D and real-time practice: Blender, Maya, Houdini, Unreal, and Unity are the controllable production tools around generative experimentation.

The portfolio presents this as hybrid creative production. It does not claim model training, fine-tuning, or other ML engineering work that is not documented.
