Production Problem

The project needed to translate a soft, cinematic cloud language into playable real-time scenes. The TD challenge was to preserve the visual intention of concept art while building systems that could be controlled, debugged, iterated, and run interactively.

Ideation / TD Approach

I began by separating the visual goal into controllable technical parts: volume, softness, motion, transformation, timing, and camera readability. I then prototyped shader / raymarching solutions, exposed parameters for iteration, and connected the systems to blendshape animation and virtual camera blocking.

Workflow

Step 01

Visual Targets

Translated concept art and motion references into practical technical targets for cloud shape, density, softness, and movement.

Step 02

Shader / Raymarching Prototype

Built raymarching and shader tests in Unity to find a real-time visual language for soft volumetric forms.

Step 03

Artist Controls

Exposed adjustable parameters so timing, density, transformation, and scene feel could be iterated without rebuilding the system.

Step 04

Animation & Camera Blocking

Connected blendshape-driven animation to shot timing, virtual camera placement, and cinematic scene composition.

Step 05

Debugging & Playable Delivery

Iterated visuals and performance so the scenes could function as stable playable real-time environments.
