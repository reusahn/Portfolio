CONTROLLED BENCHMARK

Quality advantage was consistent. Throughput behavior was not.

Splatfacto achieved higher held-out PSNR, SSIM, and lower LPIPS on both scenes. However, evaluator throughput changed by scene: Nerfacto was slightly higher on Bonsai, while Splatfacto was substantially higher on Garden. This makes the benchmark useful as a systems result, not a simplistic “GS always wins” claim.

About

I am a Technical Artist and graphics researcher working across real-time 3D, digital humans, XR, and neural graphics. My current research direction is centered on practical 3D/4D representations: how quickly they can be trained, how compactly they can be represented, how robustly they reconstruct held-out views, and how those representations behave in real-time systems.
