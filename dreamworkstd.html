This page highlights four selected production-facing works: digital human pipelines, repeatable MetaHuman workflows, mocap cleanup / retargeting, real-time graphics, debugging, artist support, and performance-aware delivery.
My Role

I work as a creative technologist and technical artist, building the bridge between visual intention and production systems. My projects often begin with an artist or director's image, character, or emotional target, then become a reproducible workflow through rigging, capture, shader systems, compositing, real-time engines, and technical iteration.

  • Built digital human and MetaHuman production workflows for real people and exhibition characters
  • Cleaned mocap data and retargeted performances across MotionBuilder, Maya, UE5, and Unity
  • Developed Unity shader / raymarching systems and real-time cinematic sequences
  • Supported artists and directors through debugging, scene setup, technical documentation, and iteration
  • Organized production-facing assets, reference data, character setups, and technical notes for repeatable artist workflows
