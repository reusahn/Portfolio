Directed By AI

A fictional family is assembled from one person's face, voice, and a generative image process.

TOOLS / MEDIUMDiffusion Models · Language Models · Voice Transformation · Film Direction
ORIGINAL PROJECT VIDEO · VIMEOVIDEO DEMONSTRATION / DIRECTED WORK
01 / THE IDEA

When the same source identity becomes several fictional people, where does authorship—and perceived reality—reside?

Directed By AI is a film and installation experiment that uses my own facial and vocal source material to create a fictional mother and sister. AI-assisted writing, diffusion-generated character imagery, and transformed voices contribute to a story centered on a mother's fear of passing illness to her daughter.

02 / EXPERIENCE

What people see, do, and experience

Viewers encounter two apparently distinct members of a family whose images are derived from one person's data. The resemblance is not announced through a conventional technical demonstration; it becomes part of the unease inside a dramatic situation.

The work combines a narrative film with generated video portraits for an installation context. Family distance, fear, and inherited vulnerability provide the story rather than acting as an excuse to showcase AI imagery.

The story is experienced as a moving image with authored pacing, voice, and performance rather than as a live generative interface.

03 / MY CONTRIBUTION

From concept to implementation

I conceived and directed the work, using facial reference data as the source for multiple character identities and combining generated portraits, voice transformation, and film composition.

Diffusion-based workflows expanded and varied the characters' appearances; language-model assistance contributed to iterations of dialogue and narrative structure. I selected and assembled the material into the finished presentation.

I altered my own recorded voice for the fictional family members. The completed experience depends on editing, staging, and story decisions beyond the model output.

05 / CREATIVE JUDGMENT

Why these choices mattered

I intentionally used one source face across different fictional identities so the film's representational premise could be felt within the characters themselves.

The important technical choice is in service of drama: the identity transform makes the relationships unfamiliar while allowing the family narrative to remain legible. AI does not replace the director's responsibility for characterization and the final sequence.

WHAT THIS PROJECT MAKES TANGIBLE

The work demonstrates authorship over a generative-media workflow: transforming a capability into character design, story, and a finished moving-image experience rather than treating generated frames as the endpoint.
