02 / THE WAY I WORK

Research is clearer when you can experience the question.

These projects cross disciplines, but share one production logic: a question becomes a model of interaction, then a system, then a demonstration that makes the possibility—and the limitation—understandable.

01 / IMAGINE

Begin with a perceptual or narrative question.

Why does an artificial being seem vulnerable? Can an NPC become part of a place? Can a generated family feel narratively coherent? The question determines the form of the prototype.

02 / BUILD & REVISE

Connect code, representation and response.

Unity C#, APIs, voice synthesis, sensors, character rigs, shaders, and Unreal systems are chosen to serve a particular encounter. An initial implementation is tested against what the experience needs to convey.

03 / COMMUNICATE

Finish the experience and tell its story.

The outcome may be an installation, a VR game, a real-time character or a film. Performance, composition and visual direction are part of how an audience understands the underlying technology.

03 / BACKGROUND

Production experience, research curiosity, directorial judgment.

My technical and cinematic work are not separate practices. One enables the other.

I'm a Technical Artist, Technical Director, and filmmaker working at the intersection of real-time systems and human perception. I've built interactive museum applications, AI-mediated characters, virtual experiences, and digital-human pipelines, while directing moving-image work shaped by questions of identity and memory.

My work includes Unity C# and real-time shader development for an independent game with Erick Oh, digital-human production with artist Yaloo, and public-facing interactive commissions. At UCSB, my ongoing Media Arts & Technology research extends this production foundation toward controllable 4D human representation, neural graphics and questions of embodied interaction. Those research directions are not presented here as completed world-model training or controlled participant studies.
