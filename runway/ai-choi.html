01 / THE IDEA

What changes when the image of a person no longer only represents them, but can answer back?

AI Choi Jung-hoon reconstructs a recognizable singer as a conversational digital human. The project brings together a generated voice, a real-time 3D character, face animation, body sensing, and a GPT-based response system to study what happens when a familiar media figure becomes an interactive presence.

02 / EXPERIENCE

What people see, do, and experience

An audience member speaks to the character rather than selecting from a conventional menu. Their words enter a conversational pipeline and return as synthesized speech.

The digital performer articulates responses through facial blendshapes and lip synchronization. Azure Kinect provides spatial and body information, allowing the encounter to include movement and proximity rather than voice alone.

The recognizability of the source figure is part of the experiment: voice, behavior, and likeness carry different expectations of authenticity.

03 / MY CONTRIBUTION

From concept to implementation

I directed the technical development and built the character from portrait references using Character Creator 4 Headshot, ZBrush refinement, Maya rigging and UV preparation, then integrated the model for real-time presentation in Unity.

I connected audience speech to transcription, GPT-based response generation, ElevenLabs synthesis, and facial blendshape animation. A coroutine-driven Unity sequence coordinates the stages so conversational output can become visible performance.

Azure Kinect tracking adds physical context through gesture and depth data; the audience can be represented as a point cloud, bringing a real body into the same interaction space as the simulated one.

05 / CREATIVE JUDGMENT

Why these choices mattered

I treated linguistic output and embodied performance as a single interface problem. The timing between spoken response and facial animation affects whether the figure is perceived as responsive or as a disconnected sequence of effects.

The digital human does not need to be described as self-aware or independently learning. Its value as a prototype comes from integrating available AI capabilities into a coherent, inspectable experience.

WHAT THIS PROJECT MAKES TANGIBLE

The prototype demonstrates how model output becomes a performed interaction through coordinated character systems, sound, and sensing. The question is not whether an artificial celebrity is the original person, but how an audience recognizes or contests that representation.
