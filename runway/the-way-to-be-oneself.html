01 / THE IDEA

What does authorship look like when many possible images can be produced, but only a few should become a film?

The Way To Be Oneself is an experimental film built with generative-image tools, including ComfyUI. The work draws on synthetic representation and visual exploration to stage questions about personhood, self-image, and the distance between a recorded act and a convincing generated one.

02 / EXPERIENCE

What people see, do, and experience

The audience watches a directed moving-image work, not a collection of unrelated generation samples. The images are selected and organized for visual continuity, tone, and a sense of a character moving through time.

The film's synthetic images invite an ambiguity: a recognizable screen presence can seem to document a performance without a conventional camera having recorded that same event.

03 / MY CONTRIBUTION

From concept to implementation

I developed the film as its director, using ComfyUI and generative image workflows for visual exploration. I selected among outputs, rejected directions that did not support the intent, and refined the sequence as authored cinema.

I combined image-making decisions with editorial rhythm and continuity. The labor of direction sits in the iteration: defining what the image should do, assessing what the model produces, and deciding how a scene fits the next.

05 / CREATIVE JUDGMENT

Why these choices mattered

I treated generated material as raw footage that still needs cinematic judgment. The ability to generate another image does not answer whether it makes the film more coherent.

The project supports my broader interest in how generated media changes our sense of visual evidence, but it is a finished work of moving-image art—not a validated human-subjects experiment or a newly trained video model.

WHAT THIS PROJECT MAKES TANGIBLE

The work complements interactive projects with a different kind of prototyping evidence: visual investigation, rapid iteration, editorial judgment, and the ability to package experimental media into a compelling moving image.
