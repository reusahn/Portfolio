One focused research instrument / source prototype

Context Gate

A hint is generated. The person keeps moving. Is the hint still applicable when it arrives?

I am building a Unity harness that compares unconditional delivery with checks on a hint’s object-state preconditions. Conditions, trial boundaries, and delivery decisions are recorded.

How I work

Make the behavior visible.
Keep the control explicit.

The useful artifact is an interactive system another person can inspect, modify, and run. I organize the technical cases around the boundary I worked on.

  1. Translate intent into a controllable systemChoose the input, state, and feedback that carry the experience.
  2. Inspect the difficult boundarySeparate rendering from collision, processing from display, and model output from game rules.
  3. Make claims traceableConnect the project archive, new code examples, and the exact captures still required.

I am a PhD researcher in Media Arts and Technology at UC Santa Barbara and hold an MFA in Art and Technology from CalArts. I work across games, XR, digital humans, and interactive exhibitions.

My practice starts with how a person encounters a system: what they can do, how it responds, and what that response means. I use Unity and C# to turn those questions into interactive environments.

My Unreal and MetaHuman work on Shininho and Jooan adds character-production experience. It is documented separately from the Unity systems presented here.
