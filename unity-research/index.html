A practice across art and engineering

The question and
the system belong together.

I am an artist, developer, and PhD researcher in Media Arts and Technology at UC Santa Barbara. I hold an MFA in Art and Technology from CalArts.

I work with the conditions of an encounter: what someone can do, how a system perceives that action, and how its response becomes meaningful. Unity and C# let me translate those questions into real-time experiences.

The projects include collaborations, directed works, and technical-development roles. Each case identifies my contribution. Unreal and MetaHuman work on Shininho and Jooan is documented separately.
