What I built in Unity / existing implementation

Map webcam input into a particle field.

I mapped live webcam imagery into a Unity VFX Graph system. The incoming image supplies the material for a bead-like visual field, making the relation between the camera, the moving person, and the display immediately visible.

01 / Webcam · Unity image input

Connect a live webcam image to the visual system

I connected webcam input to the Unity work. The changing camera image supplies the visual system with a live source. The implementation should be read as a camera-to-image mapping, rather than assuming a depth camera, skeleton tracking, or a segmentation algorithm that has not been confirmed.

02 / VFX Graph · image mapping · particles

Map the image into VFX Graph output

I developed the webcam mapping into a bead-like particle field in VFX Graph. The essential connection is between the source image and the visible particle structure. The exact sampling and property bindings still need the original graph capture, rather than a guessed implementation based on the final appearance.

03 / Particle appearance · material behavior · display

Keep a person's action legible through abstraction

The bead treatment changes the image while retaining enough structure for people to recognize their relation to the display. I implemented the real-time visual system around that mapping. The particle image and the physical presentation contribute different parts of the encounter.

Technical structure & design decisions

How the parts connect.

01

Image input and particle output

The important boundary is the mapping from a camera frame to the VFX Graph result. The image supplies a changing source rather than a sequence of pre-rendered effects.

02

Legibility within abstraction

The visual treatment has to preserve enough structure for a visitor to connect a movement with its visible trace. Particle form and image correspondence carry different parts of that feedback.

The conceptual starting point

What remains of a person's presence when a camera image becomes a field of discrete visual units?

The installation treats an ordinary encounter with a camera as a temporary act of image-making. A figure can remain recognizable while its surface becomes fragmented and tactile. The work brings the passing visitor into the composition without requiring a separate instruction-heavy interface.

Pre-production & development logic

From intent to interaction.

A retrospective design note based on the documented work. Newly prepared for this portfolio.

  1. 01

    Choose the encounter

    Begin with people passing through a camera's view rather than a formal start button.

  2. 02

    Set the mapping

    Compare the live source and its particle representation. Decide which image relationships should remain readable.

  3. 03

    Judge the display

    Assess recognition, scale, and movement from where a passerby actually sees the work.
