To Eternity

2020 · Coding director / Unity developer

Azure Kinect · Timeline · Live compositing

I connected Azure Kinect sensing to Unity and integrated live interaction with Timeline-based media sequencing. Body input, archival-image composition, and timed presentation work together so the memorial responds to the participant rather than playing as a fixed video.

Unity implementation

Azure Kinect input

I connected Azure Kinect sensing to the Unity installation. The participant's body supplies live input for the encounter instead of acting only as a subject in a prerecorded video. The meaningful boundary is where sensor data becomes the participation event used by the application.

Timeline integration

I integrated sensor-driven interaction with Timeline-based presentation. Authored media has its own timing, while a participant acts at an unpredictable moment. The implementation brings those two timelines together so an input can lead into the memorial sequence and its screen transitions.

Live image composition

I integrated the live participant image with archival-image composition and the timed presentation. The resulting memorial image connects a present body with historical material. Sensing, the image composition, and the sequence remain separate parts of the technical explanation so each can be inspected.

System & design decisions

A sensor event inside a timed sequence

Timeline organizes authored media over time, while the sensor introduces events that arrive according to a participant's actions. Integrating those two modes is the central implementation problem.

Live presence and archival material

The participant's body supplies a changing visual structure for historical imagery. Tracking, composition, and sequence state must remain understandable as separate stages.

Verification and reuse notes

These are newly prepared checks, not reported production incidents.

Design decision
Keep live sensor input distinct from authored Timeline time. The integration connects participant activity to a media sequence while preserving control over the composition.

Observable evidence

Reuse boundary

Concept

How can a person enter an archive through a gesture rather than only look at it?

The work commemorates the Gwangju Democratization Movement. Its central relation is between a person present now and photographs of people and events in the past. The archive is carried by the visitor's silhouette, making participation an act of attention and remembrance.
