Why there are no benchmark claims

A number needs
a measured environment.

The portfolio does not invent frame rates, response latency, uptime, participant counts, or study effects. Any later performance comparison should retain the build, hardware, test scene, configuration, and before/after trace.

An archive is a starting point.
