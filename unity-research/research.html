Tobias Höllerer / Four Eyes Lab

Experimental XR systems
and observable interaction.

The official profile describes HCI, experimental systems, AR/VR, interactive visualization, and real-time graphics. Recent publications add concrete examples of controlled spatial tasks and XR authoring tools.

SABER: Spatial Attention, Brain, Extended Reality · IEEE VR 2026 ↗

The paper uses Unity to run a controlled VR task and coordinate behavioral, eye-tracking, and EEG records. It makes trial control and event timing directly relevant to implementation quality.

XARP: A Human-First and Agent-Ready Extended Reality Toolkit in Python · EICS 2026 ↗

XARP separates Python application logic from a Unity client. It is a concrete context for work on client-side interactions, lifecycle behavior, and reusable diagnostic examples.

My candidate contribution

Unity-side visual-feedback controls and an auditable task lifecycle. To Eternity provides sensor-to-sequence implementation context. Erick Oh provides shader-control context. Context Gate makes a delayed-response policy explicit in independent source.

Misha Sra / Human–AI Experience Lab

AI assistance situated
in a changing world.

The official profile frames spatial human–AI interaction around shared structured context. Its current work includes assistance timing, XR platforms, embodied applications, and methods for human–AI coordination.

How Users Perceive Mixed-Initiative AI · IUI 2026 ↗

The study compares on-demand and idle-timer help in a structured puzzle task. It uses a deterministic solver to hold assistance quality consistent while examining delivery modes.

A design toolkit for task support with mixed reality and artificial intelligence · 2025 ↗

The design framework makes the actor, intended effect, and interaction considerations explicit. Timing and error handling are useful lenses for framing an implementation case.

My candidate contribution

Unity testbeds that connect a response to a body, a task, and a social interpretation. AI ZOO connects language with physical output. AI Choi Jung-hoon makes a reply into character performance. Escape Metro gives dialogue a puzzle condition.
