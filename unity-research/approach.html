Development approach

A technique matters because of the encounter it makes possible. I read the work through six decisions that connect an artistic premise to an interactive system.

This framework and the project development notes were written for this portfolio. They reconstruct the logic of the documented works and identify useful next artifacts. They do not stand in for original production records.

  1. 01

    Frame the relation

    What changes between the person and the system?

    Fineo turns classification into a world. AI ZOO stages a relation of apparent confinement. To Eternity connects a present body to an archive.

    Fineo ↗
  2. 02

    Choose the participant's action

    The available action is part of the concept.

    Speaking, moving, painting, waiting, and simply passing through a camera's view each create a different kind of encounter.

    NARCI / Be NARCI ↗
  3. 03

    Define the translation

    Make the mapping explicit.

    Decide what a sensed movement, image, sound, or model response can change. Keep the input, interpretation, and visible output distinct enough to inspect.

    Jemulpo Photo Studio ↗
  4. 04

    Build a controllable slice

    One complete interaction before a larger apparatus.

    A shader with useful controls, one sensor-to-character response, or a complete spoken turn exposes the difficult boundary before the whole experience is assembled.

    Erick Oh collaboration ↗
  5. 05

    Rehearse at the encounter's scale

    An Editor view is one part of the evidence.

    Inspect the body in the tracking area, the character on its display, the sound in the venue, or the world in the headset. Capture the actual input-to-response sequence.

    To Eternity ↗
  6. 06

    Separate reflection from results

    A good question can remain open.

    The artwork makes an encounter possible. A study adds a controlled comparison and an analysis. Keep an artistic intention, an implementation, and a measured finding distinguishable.

    Context Gate ↗
