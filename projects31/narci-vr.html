Embodied interaction as world behavior

NARCI places the viewer inside a deliberately restricted spatial environment where reflections, proximity, and gesture shape the experience. The technical problem was not just scene construction; the environment had to respond coherently to a body inside it.

Technical implementation

  • Built the experience in Unity using XR Interaction Toolkit.
  • Used headset hand tracking for touch, pinch, and grab-oriented interaction without relying on controllers.
  • Mapped tracked input into custom interaction states in C#.
  • Built reflective / mirror-style visual behavior and material transitions for the enclosed environment.
  • Iterated lighting, scale, pacing, and spatial staging in-headset rather than treating VR as a final export target.
Hand / Headset Tracking ↓ Unity XR Input ↓ C# Interaction State ↓ Material / Spatial Response ↓ Embodied User Feedback

Why this matters for VRChat

NARCI demonstrates world-level technical art: interaction is tied directly to material behavior, spatial perception, and headset input. It shows experience with the part of VR production where scene content and runtime systems stop being separable.
