Gang Se-hwang Digital Human

A custom historical digital human reconstructed from portrait evidence, prepared for facial and body performance, integrated into Unity, and deployed as an interactive museum experience.

ROLE Technical Director FOCUS Custom character asset Facial / body performance Unity integration Interactive deployment

Asset-to-runtime ownership

The character was built as a custom production asset rather than a stock character. Portrait-based reconstruction moved through topology, UVs, skin and hair assets, facial deformation, performance data, costume delivery, and final Unity integration.

Unity-side implementation

  • Imported the character, animation, materials, hair, and costume assets into Unity.
  • Implemented interactive animation triggers and presentation behavior through C#.
  • Integrated speech-driven and proximity-driven responses for a real-time museum docent.
  • Validated the final experience as a deployed installation rather than an Editor-only character showcase.

Why it matters for a content pipeline

This project demonstrates the handoff points that frequently break real-time content: topology and skinning, animation compatibility, materials, asset import, runtime state, and final installation behavior. My role was to keep those layers coherent from source asset through deployed Unity experience.
