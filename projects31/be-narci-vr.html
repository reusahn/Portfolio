One technical foundation, a different experience

Be NARCI was developed as a related but separate Unity scene. Instead of rebuilding the technical stack from scratch, it reused shared shader, voice, and interaction assets while changing the participant’s role and the way world content reacted.

Technical implementation

  • Reused shared Unity assets and project conventions across separate scenes.
  • Mapped player interaction to runtime material-state changes.
  • Used shader-driven transitions to move figures between different surface states.
  • Maintained common technical assets while allowing the companion experience to diverge visually and behaviorally.
Shared Unity Assets ↓ Scene-Specific Interaction ↓ Runtime Material Parameters ↓ Visual Transformation ↓ Different Player Role / Experience

Why this matters for VRChat

The useful evidence here is reuse. Live content pipelines benefit when multiple worlds or events can share stable technical building blocks while remaining visually distinct. Be NARCI demonstrates that mindset at a smaller project scale.
