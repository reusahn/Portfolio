NARCI

An immersive psychological VR scene built around a confined elevator environment, reflective surfaces, headset interaction, and carefully controlled spatial staging.

2019 · UNITY VR / INTERACTIVE SURFACE

Graffiti Freedom

A virtual subway environment where the player paints directly onto world surfaces rather than interacting with a pre-authored static prop.

  • Particle-based spray interaction.
  • Collision-driven paint coordinates written into a dynamic render texture.
  • Custom wall shader reads the paint mask so user input becomes persistent world content.
  • Clear separation between interaction logic, particle feedback, and surface rendering.
