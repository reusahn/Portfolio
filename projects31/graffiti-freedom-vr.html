Graffiti Freedom

Making the environment writable

The core system lets a player spray paint directly onto world geometry. Instead of swapping a decal or triggering a canned animation, interaction data is converted into persistent texture information used by the wall material.

Technical implementation

  • Built particle-based spray feedback in Unity.
  • Used collision points to determine where paint should land on a surface.
  • Updated a dynamic RenderTexture from player interaction.
  • Used a custom wall shader to read the evolving paint mask.
  • Separated input / collision, particle feedback, texture state, and final material rendering into distinct runtime responsibilities.
VR Input ↓ Spray Particles ↓ Surface Collision Coordinates ↓ Dynamic RenderTexture ↓ Wall Shader Paint Mask ↓ Persistent Modified World Surface

Why this matters for VRChat

This is directly relevant to social-world content because the environment is not merely viewed; users alter it. The system demonstrates technical art across interaction, VFX, shader logic, and persistent visual state.
