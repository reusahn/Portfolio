CHARACTER DEVELOPMENT LENS

I do not treat a generated image or a solved face as a finished character.

Across these projects, the design work happens in the decisions between references and production: what proportions define the character, which expression vocabulary is needed, what should change structurally versus materially, what visual information survives across views and shots, and where AI exploration should stop so authored 3D decisions can take over.

01 / FORMIdentity, proportion, silhouette

Historical reconstruction, age variation, real-person likeness, body proportion changes, and multi-view validation.

02 / PERFORMANCEExpression and emotional range

52-channel ARKit foundations, facial blendshapes, MetaHuman performance, capture cleanup, and readable expression under final lighting.

03 / PRODUCTIONConsistency across the pipeline

Hair, costume, materials, rigs, retargeting, AI visual exploration, engine implementation, technical notes, and iterative troubleshooting.

AI-ASSISTED WORKFLOW

AI expands the search space; the artist still owns the character.

I use generative tools to produce alternatives quickly, then apply traditional visual judgment and production constraints. Selected directions move into Photoshop / DCC / real-time workflows where anatomy, proportion, expressions, materials, performance, and shot consistency can be deliberately controlled.

01Research

Story, references, anatomy, personality, visual constraints.

02Generate

ComfyUI / diffusion branching and visual alternatives.

03Evaluate

Compare, reject, annotate, refine, identify what carries intent.

04Design in 3D

Proportion, topology, hair, costume, surfaces, rig / face requirements.

05Perform / Test

Expressions, motion, camera, lighting, emotional range, consistency.

06Iterate

Creative notes become technical action items and pipeline improvements.

ABOUT
