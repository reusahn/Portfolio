EXPERIMENTAL GENERATIVE FILM / DIRECTED BY JONGHOON AHN · 2025

The Way To Be Oneself

A film built with synthesized images of my own face. Instead of documenting a physical performance, it constructs a screen presence through generation, selection, and cinematic sequencing.

Role · Director / ArtistTools · ComfyUI / Generative-image workflowsMedium · Experimental moving image

What appears on screen

A recognizable face performs in a film even though the depicted performance was not captured as conventional footage of the filmmaker.

What I made

I directed the work and used generative imagery to develop its filmed self-representation, evaluating variations, rejecting weak outputs, and editing the selected material into a coherent sequence.

What it asks

When a generated image depicts someone convincingly, how readily does the audience read it as evidence that the person performed the action? This is a practice-based question, not a completed audience experiment.

01 / FROM GENERATION TO DIRECTION

The authorial work happens between outputs.

My process begins with a filmed premise and a visual direction. ComfyUI provides variations, but a coherent moving image still depends on choosing what to keep, controlling contrast and visual continuity, and deciding how one image leads into the next. The system does not make those narrative decisions on its own.

02 / REPRESENTATION & PERCEPTION

A convincing image is not the same as a witnessed event.

The film extends my interest in digital humans and machine-mediated identity. A synthetic face can remain visually continuous across shots while separating the represented person's identity from an actual recorded performance. This distinction links my cinematic practice to ongoing questions about authenticity, authorship, and how people judge generated representations.
