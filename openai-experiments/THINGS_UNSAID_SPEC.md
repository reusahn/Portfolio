# The Things We Didn't Say — Project Two (Design Specification)
**Status:** planned, not built; starts after One Scene reaches a documented vertical slice.

## Premise
A visitor chooses or writes a fictional unsent message. The experience explores how small changes in language, silence, facial expression and camera distance influence its perceived emotional intent. Different expressions do **not** claim to reveal or replace the speaker's true feelings.

## Narrative interaction
1. Start with a short, optional fictional prompt (e.g., 'I was there. I didn't know how to stay.').
2. Select modes: **literal**, **hesitant**, **indirect** — initial outputs authored, with disclosed model mode planned.
3. Visitor adjusts one word, silence length or camera distance, not simply chooses a canned scene.
4. A consented synthetic actor / avatar performs the result with bounded, predictable animations; use original voice or licensed synthetic voice.
5. Compare text intention vs observed performance, reveal the system's expressive decisions.
6. Optional reflection: 'Which part still feels like yours?' No mandatory personal disclosure.

## Why an interactive AI experiment
The model can propose alternate delivery strategies, pause placement, interpretations and optional screenplay actions, but the viewer always maintains final edit control. The formal subject is the gap between intention, delegated expression and the audience's reading.

## Scoping
**MVP:** browser-based staged performance with captions, simple face/shape poses and voice optional.
**V2:** Unity / Unreal digital-human performance with MetaHuman or an original licensed character; authored blendshape/gesture controls.
**V3:** constrained model suggestions through secure server; consent, moderation, opt-out, no unnecessary storage.
**Film-art finish:** carefully timed silence and shot composition, not generic talking-head AI output.

## Safety and art direction
- No recreating real people, deceased people, or private conversations without specific rights/consent.
- Avoid automatically responding as a lost or deceased person; do not suggest AI generated performance is a person's authentic thought.
- Use privacy-first defaults; no collection of vulnerable confessions necessary.
- Distinguish original cinematic direction, AI proposals and audience modifications.
