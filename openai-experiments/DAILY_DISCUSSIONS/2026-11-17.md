## Bounded improvement — One Scene, Many Truths
Added Left/Right arrow and Home/End keyboard navigation directly to filmstrip buttons. The handler prevents default only for those four keys, stops timed playback, re-renders the selected shot, and focuses its replacement button. At boundaries the selected shot remains unchanged, while playback still stops. Existing click/Enter/Space activation remains unchanged.
