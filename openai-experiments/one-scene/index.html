AN INTERACTIVE CINEMATIC STUDY · VERSION 0.1

ONE SCENE,
MANY TRUTHS.

The event stays the same.
The story changes.

A person leaves a letter on a café table. Three witnesses see the same moment and tell themselves different stories. Choose whose perspective directs the edit — then reveal what actually happened.

AUTHOR-CRAFTED INTERACTIVE STORYBOARDThis early prototype uses pre-written narrative data and abstract vector visuals. It is not AI-generated footage or a live model demo. Model-driven edits and original filmed footage are planned, not completed.
