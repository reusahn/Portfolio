# Three Fictional Reviewers — Decision Rubric
**Purpose:** make review actionable and demanding, never imply hiring outcomes or expert endorsements.

## A — OpenAI creative hiring reviewer (100)
- Job fit: 35 (frontier AI curiosity, concept-to-delivery ownership, cross-functional cooperation).
- Portfolio clarity: 25 (hook understood within 10 seconds; source links, accessible demo, transparent contributions).
- Feasibility and evidence: 20 (running prototype, realistic production scope, tests, honest claims).
- Career continuity: 20 (film, digital human, XR and interactive exhibition connected through a coherent practice).

## B — Hypothetical OpenAI Creative, Experiments lead (100)
- AI necessity: 30 (model does work that a pre-written branching script cannot trivially replace; agency and limits visible).
- Conceptual creativity: 25 (singular expressive question; surprising interpretation; fresh form).
- Audience experience: 25 (distinct participation, immediate feedback, emotional/cultural relevance, accessibility).
- Iteration/scale: 20 (measured tests, failure handling, repeatable pipeline, production finish).

## C — Hypothetical senior AI arts critic/curator (100)
- Originality: 35 (specific contribution beyond generative content).
- Artistic thesis: 30 (clarity, stakes, sensitivity to contradictory perspectives).
- Medium experimentation: 20 (formal relation between interaction, editing and technical model behavior).
- Ethics and critique: 15 (agency, privacy, disclosure, avoiding false emotional inference).

## Acceptance gate for prototype
- [ ] A stranger can explain the core mechanic without help after one interaction.
- [ ] Every perspective preserves an immutable event ledger.
- [ ] Edits reveal **which choices changed** (shot/order/caption/audio) rather than falsely asserting multiple factual truths.
- [ ] The experience includes at least one user choice beyond simply choosing one of three canned outputs.
- [ ] Model invocation (if present) is transparent; graceful local offline mode is labeled.
- [ ] At least five consented observation sessions are logged with clear methodology, *not invented*.
- [ ] The user has tested keyboard, mobile, captions and reduced motion.
- [ ] Source, setup, media rights and failure cases are documented.

## Review cadence
At each meaningful release:
1. Reviewer A asks what a recruiter sees in the first 30 seconds.
2. Reviewer B tries to reproduce the experience, investigates model necessity and technical quality.
3. Reviewer C interrogates originality, ethics and expressive coherence.
4. Record **specific failing criteria**, make edits, then rescore — never automatically assign 100.
