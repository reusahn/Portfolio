SplatStream Lab · 3DGS · PyTorch / CUDA

Compression, quality, and streaming for interactive 3D Gaussian Splatting

Built a reproducible full-scene rate-distortion pipeline on Mip-NeRF 360 / Bonsai using pruning, FP16/INT8 quantization, compact payload accounting, and held-out CUDA rendering. The selected 25% pruning + FP16 operating point reduces the measured zlib-backed payload from 185.43 MiB to 70.04 MiB while retaining 27.6234 dB PSNR, 0.9007 SSIM, and 0.1659 LPIPS.

Interactive Digital Humans · HUGS Baseline v1

Interaction-ready 4D human representations

Completed a locality/leakage baseline that perturbs SMPL joints in HUGS and measures whether Gaussian displacement remains within the expected kinematic subtree. The current Seattle/frame-0 study contains 36 saved probe measurements over 472,958 human Gaussians; observed articulation-dependent leakage includes 11.21% at left-wrist z and 26.57% at left-ankle z. Results are scoped to one sequence/frame.

Ray-Scene Acceleration · C++17

BVH / SAH / grid acceleration implemented from scratch

Implemented brute-force ray-triangle intersection, median BVH, binned-SAH BVH, uniform grid, 3D-DDA, instrumentation, and differential correctness tests. On a 99,972-triangle scene with 50,000 rays, binned-SAH BVH measured 64.6 ms vs 28,665 ms brute force (444x), with 12.8 vs 99,972 triangle tests/ray. Test suite: 34 checks, 0 failures.
